Human-GEM Pruning Atlas

Each dot is one node of the network at one stage of pruning. Across is its number of edges. Up is the share of current it carries when random metabolites act as sources and targets, with no data involved. Colour is how many metabolites would be cut off from the rest of the network without it. Dots high on the left, and dark blue dots, are bottlenecks made by wiring: any analysis that measures a metabolite behind them will push current through them whatever the biology.

    One map for every stage. Each node keeps its place, so switching stages shows what pruning does: hubs fade out on the left once they are removed, blue pockets appear as bottlenecks form, and the islands on the right lose their last links to the main network. Only the nodes this page is about are drawn: pocket nodes and their pockets, the busiest hubs, and islands of four or more metabolites.
